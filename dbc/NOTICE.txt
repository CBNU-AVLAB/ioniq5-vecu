CAN matrix definitions (dbc/) — third-party material, published by permission
================================================================================

WHAT THESE FILES ARE

  ADA_S.dbc, ADA_B.dbc, ADE_A.dbc describe the CAN matrix of the ADA-S
  (steering), ADA-B (braking) and ADE-A (accelerator pedal emulator) actuator
  controllers. They were generated from the controller supplier's CAN matrix
  specification documents. The original specification documents are not
  included in this repository.
